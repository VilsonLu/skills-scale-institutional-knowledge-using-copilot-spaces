# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads translate product direction into technical execution. They guide architecture decisions, team capacity planning, and delivery coordination across engineering workstreams.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical direction and architecture for the project or feature area
- Break work into manageable delivery plans with developers and QA partners
- Identify dependencies, technical risks, and sequencing constraints
- Coordinate with Project Managers on milestones, resourcing, and delivery health
- Support design review and technical decision-making

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality software that meets product intent
- Maintain coherent technical standards and architecture
- Improve predictability and throughput across the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Sprint and milestone health updates
- Cross-team dependency discussions and escalation paths

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to translate backlog items into implementation plans and technical design choices.
- Partners with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and sequencing decisions.
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align schedule, resourcing, and risk management.

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, validation strategies, and release readiness criteria so defects are caught early and customer-facing quality remains high.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, automation priorities, and acceptance validation criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated test coverage with developers and stakeholders
- Track quality risks, defects, and release readiness across milestones
- Partner with product and engineering teams on validation plans and sign-off
- Support continuous improvement of test processes and release quality gates

### Goals
- Reduce product risk and defect leakage into production
- Ensure user-facing quality matches acceptance criteria and customer expectations
- Improve confidence in release readiness and operational stability

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews, test planning, and release readiness check-ins
- Defect triage and regression discussions
- Sign-off on critical user journeys and production readiness

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate feature quality and catch issues before release.
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align QA timelines, dependencies, and release gating.
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria and customer requirements are validated.

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors set strategic direction, funding priorities, and executive support for critical initiatives. They help unblock major decisions and ensure the project aligns with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business priority and expected outcomes of the initiative
- Provide sponsorship, executive visibility, and decision support for escalations
- Align project goals with broader business strategy and organizational priorities
- Resolve cross-functional conflicts or funding constraints when needed
- Support communication with leadership and other stakeholder groups

### Goals
- Ensure strategic alignment and business value realization
- Protect focus on the most important outcomes and milestones
- Enable timely decisions when cross-team or executive support is needed

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee meetings and leadership briefings
- Strategic milestone reviews and business risk updates
- Escalations for major blockers or business-impacting decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers to confirm priorities and milestones.
- Provides direction and support to Engineering Leads during executive escalations or major scope decisions.
- Works with stakeholders to ensure continued sponsorship and alignment across the project lifecycle.

---

## Customer Success / Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Adoption Leads ensure that customer experience, onboarding, and ongoing value realization are considered throughout delivery. They help connect product work to user outcomes and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, pain points, and adoption barriers
- Align launch plans, support readiness, and rollout communications with product teams
- Monitor post-release adoption, usage signals, and user sentiment
- Help define success criteria for customer experience and operational readiness
- Coordinate feedback loops between customers, support, and delivery teams

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption, retention, and satisfaction
- Ensure releases are operationally ready and usable in real-world scenarios
- Connect product delivery to measurable customer value

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions, adoption reviews, and readiness check-ins
- Launch support coordination and stakeholder updates
- Feedback loops across support, sales, and delivery teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer needs and success metrics.
- Shares adoption and readiness insights with Project Managers and Engineering Leads to inform release planning.
- Supports Developers and QA teams by clarifying user expectations and business-critical workflows.

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners help ensure projects meet required controls, privacy expectations, and security standards without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risks related to security, privacy, compliance, and data handling
- Define required controls, review processes, and approval gates
- Support secure design decisions and incident response readiness
- Verify that deliverables meet regulatory or organizational requirements
- Partner with engineering and project teams on risk mitigation plans

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the lifecycle
- Improve trust, resilience, and audit readiness
- Support safe and compliant delivery without compromising product value

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, design risk assessments, and compliance check-ins
- Escalation of vulnerabilities or policy gaps
- Approval and remediation planning for sensitive or regulated work

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Engineering Leads and Developers on secure architecture and implementation standards.
- Collaborates with Project Managers on dependency tracking, approvals, and release gating.
- Supports Product Managers and Sponsors in understanding risk trade-offs and needed controls for strategic initiatives.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together these roles help clarify accountability, improve cross-functional handoffs, and strengthen delivery outcomes across planning, execution, and release.
