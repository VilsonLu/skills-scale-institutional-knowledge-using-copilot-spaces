# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository brings together the project management guidance used by OctoAcme so teams can consistently initiate, plan, deliver, and improve work. It is designed as a practical reference for project leads, product stakeholders, and engineering teams who need a shared operating model for cross-functional work. The documents in this folder define the expected rhythm of delivery, communication patterns, quality standards, and governance checkpoints that help teams stay aligned on outcomes.

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle that begins with project initiation and ends with continuous improvement. During initiation, teams confirm the business problem, define stakeholders, document success metrics, and decide whether the initiative is ready to move into planning. Planning then turns the idea into an actionable backlog, with prioritized work, estimated effort, milestones, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Execution focuses on day-to-day delivery through standups, work tracking, reviews, and issue escalation so teams can move steadily and transparently toward milestones.

The delivery model emphasizes clear roles and strong communication. Product and project leads align on priorities and business outcomes, while developers build and validate the work against quality expectations. Regular status updates, stakeholder communication, and escalation paths ensure that blockers and risks are surfaced early. Risk management includes documenting impact, likelihood, owners, and mitigation plans so teams can respond proactively rather than reactively. Throughout delivery, quality assurance is embedded in the workflow through tests, CI checks, security scanning, and manual validation when needed.

When a release is ready, teams follow a standardized deployment and rollback process to verify quality before production and to respond quickly if something goes wrong. After each milestone or release, team members capture lessons learned in retrospectives and turn the most valuable improvements into action items. This cadence keeps the operating model practical, repeatable, and focused on learning as the team matures.

## How to Use This Repo

Use this folder as the entry point for project governance, team alignment, and day-to-day execution. Start with the overview and initiation guidance for new work, move into planning and execution documents as the project progresses, and refer to release, risk, and retrospective guides when managing delivery health. Together, these documents provide a practical framework for delivering value while maintaining quality, accountability, and continuous improvement.
